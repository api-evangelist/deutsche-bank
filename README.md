Deutsche Bank is a global financial institution headquartered in Frankfurt, providing retail, corporate and investment banking, asset management and wealth management. Its developer programme - dbAPI, published at developer.db.com - goes well beyond the PSD2 regulatory minimum: 36 first-party OpenAPI 3.0.x contracts covering 195 operations across banking, cards, payments, investments, lending, onboarding, reference data and merchant acquiring, all retrievable from Deutsche Bank's own public swagger catalogue. The same contracts serve three tenants - Deutsche Bank, norisbank and Postbank. Access is OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect with 42 published scopes, PSD2 strong customer authentication exposed as its own API, a free self-service simulation gateway with persona-based test users, and a reviewed go-live process for production data.

**Areas:** Banking · Finance · Wealth Management · Open Banking · PSD2 · Payments · SEPA · Investment · Credit Cards · Merchant Solutions · Germany · Financial Services

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Deutsche Bank: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
